03 / RULES ENGINE

What Deckforge checks

Deck size

At least 60 cards in constructed formats; exactly 100 including a commander in Commander.

Copy limits

Up to four of a card by English name, except basic lands and cards whose text says otherwise.

Commander identity

Every card must fit the commander's color identity; the commander must be a legal commander.

Format legality

For cards fetched from Scryfall, current format legality is screened before building.

Deckforge is a planning assistant, not a tournament judge. Always check your event’s current banned list, companion restrictions, and individual card text before play.
